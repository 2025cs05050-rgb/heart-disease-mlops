Key findings¶

  • Size: 303 rows × 14 columns; nearly balanced classes (≈54% / 46%).
  • Missing values: Only 6 missing cells across ca and thal — handled by median/mode imputation in the preprocessing pipeline.
  • Strongest associations with the target are typically cp (chest pain type), thalach (max heart rate), oldpeak, exang, ca, and thal.
  • Scales differ widely (age ~30-80 vs oldpeak ~0-6) → standard scaling required for distance-based / linear models.
  • Categorical variables are integer-encoded but nominal (cp, restecg, slope, thal) → one-hot encode in the pipeline.
